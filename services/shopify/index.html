Shopify Development

Shopify makes it easy to start selling online, but a store that actually converts needs more than a default theme. We design and develop Shopify stores around your products and customers: clear product pages, easy navigation, fast loading and a checkout that people finish. Whether you are launching a new store or fixing one that is not selling, we build it so it is simple for you to manage every day.

What's included

What our Shopify development includes

Store setup from scratch

Products, collections, variants, payment gateways, shipping rates, taxes and policies configured correctly before launch.

Custom theme design

A Shopify theme designed for your brand, or a premium theme customised so your store does not look like every other shop.

Product pages that convert

Clear photos, benefit-led descriptions, size guides, reviews and trust badges placed where buyers look before adding to cart.

Speed and mobile performance

Optimised images, fewer heavy apps and clean Liquid code so your store loads fast on phones.

Shopify SEO

Product and collection titles, meta descriptions, clean URLs, structured data and internal links set up so products can be found on Google.

Apps and integrations

Reviews, WhatsApp, email marketing, analytics and the Meta and Google sales channels connected and tested.

Who this is for

  • Brands launching their first online store.
  • Sellers moving from Instagram or marketplaces to their own website.
  • Existing Shopify stores with low conversion or slow loading.
  • Businesses migrating from WooCommerce or another platform.

Shopify or WordPress?

Shopify is a strong choice when selling products online is the main goal: it handles hosting, security, payments and checkout reliably, and it is easy for a small team to manage orders. WordPress with WooCommerce gives more flexibility for content-heavy sites. If you are unsure, we will recommend the platform that suits your products, budget and team.

Built for everyday management

After launch, you should be able to add products, run discounts and update banners without calling a developer. We hand over a store that is organised logically and show your team how to run it.

How we work

Our Shopify development process

  1. 01

    Store planning

    We map your products, collections, variants, shipping zones and the apps you need.

  2. 02

    Design

    We design the homepage, collection and product pages around how your customers shop.

  3. 03

    Theme development

    We build or customise the theme in Liquid, keeping code light and pages fast.

  4. 04

    Setup and testing

    We configure payments, shipping and apps, then place test orders on mobile and desktop.

  5. 05

    Launch and handover

    We launch the store, connect analytics and sales channels, and train your team.
