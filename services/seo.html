What our SEO service covers

Technical SEO audit

We check how search engines crawl and index your site: broken pages, redirect chains, duplicate content, sitemap and robots.txt, canonical tags, mobile usability and structured data. You get a prioritised list of fixes, not a 90-page PDF.

Keyword research & mapping

We find the searches your customers actually make, group them by intent, and map each group to one page so your pages stop competing with each other.

On-page optimisation

Titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, image alt text and page copy, rewritten so each page clearly targets its keyword and reads naturally for visitors.

Page speed & Core Web Vitals

We compress images, trim heavy scripts and fix layout shifts so your pages load fast on mobile, which helps both rankings and conversions.

Local & Google Business Profile SEO

For businesses that serve customers in specific areas, we set up and optimise your Google Business Profile, keep your name and phone consistent across listings, and build pages for the areas you serve.

Content that ranks

Service pages, blog posts and FAQs written around real search questions, with a clear structure, internal links and a call to action on every page.

Who our SEO service is for

Our SEO work is built for small and growing businesses that want steady enquiries from search instead of relying only on paid ads. It suits service businesses, clinics, schools, online stores and B2B companies that have a website but are not showing up for the searches that matter.

  • Your website gets visitors but very few enquiries.
  • Competitors appear on Google for your services and you do not.
  • You recently redesigned your website and lost rankings.
  • You are paying for ads and want a lower-cost source of leads.

SEO that works with your website, not against it

Because we design and build websites as well, we can fix SEO problems at the source: in the code, the page structure and the content, not only in a plugin setting. Whether your site runs on WordPress, Shopify or a custom React build, we make changes that search engines can see and that do not break your design.

Every recommendation comes with a reason. You will know what we changed, why it matters, and what to expect from it.

What results to expect, and when

Technical fixes such as indexing errors, slow pages and missing metadata can show improvement within a few weeks of Google recrawling your site. Ranking for competitive keywords takes longer, usually three to six months of consistent on-page work, content and links.

We do not promise a guaranteed number one position, because no honest agency can. What we promise is clear priorities, measurable progress in Google Search Console, and work that keeps paying off after it is done.

How we work

Our SEO process

  1. 01

    Audit & baseline

    We review your site, Google Search Console and analytics to record where you rank today and what is holding you back.

  2. 02

    Keyword strategy

    We agree on the keywords and pages to focus on first, based on search demand and how likely each one is to bring an enquiry.

  3. 03

    Technical & on-page fixes

    We fix crawl and speed issues, then optimise titles, headings, content and internal links on the priority pages.

  4. 04

    Content & authority

    We publish helpful content and build genuine links and listings that make your site more trusted by search engines.

  5. 05

    Monthly reporting

    You get a plain-language report showing rankings, traffic and enquiries, plus what we will work on next.
