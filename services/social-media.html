Social Media Marketing

For many customers, your social media is the first place they check before contacting you. An inactive or inconsistent profile raises doubts, while a regular, well-designed feed builds trust. Our social media marketing service plans, creates and publishes content for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn, so your brand stays visible and your profiles turn followers into enquiries.

What our social media marketing covers

Social media strategy

We define your audience, content pillars, posting frequency and goals for each platform.

Monthly content calendar

A planned calendar of posts, carousels, reels and stories shared with you for approval before anything goes live.

Design and reels

On-brand graphics, carousels and short-form video edits designed to stop the scroll.

Captions and hashtags

Clear captions with a call to action and relevant hashtags written for each platform.

Scheduling and publishing

Posts scheduled at the times your audience is most active, so your profiles stay consistent.

Monthly reporting

A simple report on reach, engagement, follower growth and enquiries, with what we will change next month.

Platforms we manage

  • Instagram: posts, carousels, reels, stories and highlight covers.
  • Facebook: page posts, events and community updates.
  • LinkedIn: company page content and thought leadership for founders.
  • Google Business Profile: regular updates and offers.

Organic content and paid ads together

Organic posting builds credibility with people who already know you. Paid ads reach new people who do not. The two work best together: ads bring visitors to your profile, and a strong, consistent feed convinces them to get in touch. Our performance marketing service can run ads alongside your content when you are ready.

Who this is for

Our social media service suits businesses that know they should post regularly but do not have the time or in-house design skills to do it well, including clinics, schools, restaurants, service providers and product brands.

How we work

  1. 01

  4. 04

    Approve and publish
